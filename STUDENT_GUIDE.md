# 🎓 Advanced Student Dashboard Features

## Quick Start

### What's New?
Your student dashboard now includes **6 powerful widgets** to help you succeed in your placement journey:

1. **🏢 Live Drive Feed** - See upcoming company visits with automatic skill matching
2. **📋 Application Kanban** - Track your applications visually by status
3. **📢 Notification Center** - Never miss important updates
4. **💼 Interview Repository** - Learn from others' interview experiences
5. **📊 Resume Scorer** - Analyze how well your resume matches jobs
6. **🎯 Skill Gap Analyzer** - Identify skills to focus on learning

---

## Widget Descriptions

### 1️⃣ Live Drive Feed
**What it does:** Shows upcoming company recruitment visits on your campus.

**How to use:**
- See company name, visit date, location, and expected salary range
- Check if you're eligible based on your skills
- Click "Register Interest" to attend the drive
- Your registration creates a notification in your inbox

**Key Features:**
- ✓ Automatic eligibility checking (green = eligible, red = needs skills)
- ✓ Real-time company information
- ✓ Multiple filter options
- ✓ One-click registration

---

### 2️⃣ Application Kanban Board
**What it does:** Shows all your job applications organized by status.

**How to use:**
- See applications grouped in columns: Applied → Shortlisted → Interview → Selected
- Each column shows how many applications are in that stage
- Click "View" on any application for more details
- Track interview dates and locations

**Key Features:**
- ✓ Visual status tracking
- ✓ Count badges per status
- ✓ Interview date display
- ✓ Drag-and-drop ready (coming soon)

---

### 3️⃣ Notification Center
**What it does:** Keeps you informed about interviews, application updates, and opportunities.

**How to use:**
- All notifications appear grouped by priority (High, Medium, Low)
- Each notification has a type icon (📅 interview, 📝 application update, etc.)
- Click "✓ Read" to mark notifications as seen
- Unread notifications appear first

**Notification Types:**
- 📅 **Interview Scheduled** - When interview date is set
- 📝 **Application Update** - When status changes
- 🎯 **Job Match** - When a job matches your profile
- 🏢 **Company Visit** - When a drive is scheduled
- 📢 **Announcement** - Important news from placement team
- ⚡ **Skill Alert** - Skills trending in job market

---

### 4️⃣ Interview Experience Repository
**What it does:** A searchable database of interview experiences shared by students.

**How to use:**
1. Search by company name or topic (JavaScript, system design, etc.)
2. Filter by difficulty level (Easy, Medium, Hard)
3. Filter by interview type (Online, Phone, In-Person)
4. Read full experience to learn tips and tricks
5. See pass/fail outcomes and ratings

**Benefits:**
- Learn what to expect in interviews
- Get insider tips and advice
- See what topics are commonly asked
- Build confidence before your interview

---

### 5️⃣ Resume Scorer (AI Mock)
**What it does:** Analyzes how well your resume matches a job description.

**How to use:**
1. Paste the job description in the textarea
2. Click "Analyze Match"
3. Get an instant match score (0-100%)
4. See which skills you have ✓ and which you're missing ✗
5. Get recommendations to improve your score

**Score Breakdown:**
- 80-100% → Excellent match, apply now!
- 60-80% → Good match, you're competitive
- 40-60% → Decent fit, consider applying
- 0-40% → Work on skills first

---

### 6️⃣ Skill Gap Visualizer
**What it does:** Shows your proficiency vs. what the market demands.

**How to use:**
- See each skill with two bars: Your level and Market demand
- Skills with bigger gaps are higher priority to learn
- Color coded: 🟢 Green (small gap), 🟡 Amber (medium), 🔴 Red (large gap)
- Focus on learning red/amber skills to boost employability

**How it helps:**
- Know which skills are most in-demand
- Prioritize your learning
- Track your skill development
- Stay ahead of market trends

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Widgets not loading?
**Solution:** 
1. Check browser console (F12)
2. Hard refresh page (Ctrl+F5)
3. Clear browser cache
4. Try different browser

### API errors (401)?
**Solution:**
1. Re-login with your credentials
2. Check localStorage isn't cleared
3. Contact support if problem persists

### Responsive layout broken?
**Solution:**
1. Zoom out to 100% (Ctrl+0)
2. Close extra browser windows
3. Rotate device if on mobile

### Missing data in widgets?
**Solution:**
1. Click "Refresh" button on widget
2. Wait a few seconds for data to load
3. Check internet connection
4. Try again after 5 minutes
